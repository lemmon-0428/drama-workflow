──────── 正文 / Script ────────
内景。诺亚飞船大厅。继续行驶。
宇宙飞船内部大厅，老人从单个隔间走出到大厅，一个干瘦的老人对着K和机器人说。
老人：
"You know, the apocalypse is God's test for us. Just like Babel back in the day—"
男人：
"Old man, shut up."
一个女人和一个粗壮的男人也走出了舱内隔间，来到大厅。
老人（毫不在意，继续）：
"—Genesis, chapter eleven. Back then, the whole world had one language and one speech. In their arrogance, they tried to build a tower to heaven. So the Lord confused their language, so they could not understand one another. From that day—"
K（打断，声音冰冷）：
"From that day, humans learned to misunderstand."
诺亚飞船大厅舱内安静了片刻。机器人发出“嘀”的一声，像在点赞。
老人（激动地拍大腿）：
"Exactly! Exactly! The way we are now—suspicion, division, tearing each other apart—it's the continuation of Babel!"
女人（崇拜地看着老人）：
"Sir, you really know so much."
老人（眼睛亮起光芒）：
"That's why I say, we are alive to spread—"
K（猛踩刹车，车内所有人往前一倾）：
"To spread what? To stay alive."
他盯着仪表盘。上面红灯闪烁。
K：
"Not enough fuel. The Noah's Ark can only carry three people."
空气凝固。

──────── 正文 / Script ────────
内景。诺亚飞船。夜。舱窗外月光是唯一光源。
K（率先开口，声音平静）：
"Whoever consumes the most resources gets off. That's physics, not morality."
他看向老人。
老人（如被针刺，立刻反驳）：
"Me? I eat the least! And the spiritual wealth I carry—"
K：
"Spirit doesn't burn into fuel."
女人（尖叫）：
"You can't kick the old man out! He knows the most—he's our hope!"
K（看向女人，嘴角浮起一丝嘲讽）：
"Then let him get off. You take his place?"
女人的脸刷地白了。
男人（猛地站起身，头撞到车顶，怒吼）：
"Say one more fucking word, I dare you!"
K（没有回头，声音像冰刃）：
"Easy there, last Adam on earth. I'm just discussing resource allocation. Oh, and—"* 他微微侧头，"Since she's the only Eve... have you considered, for the sake of humanity, that she should—"
话没说完。男人已经把枪抵在K的后脑勺上。
男人（声音在发抖，因为被说中了最隐秘的念头）：
"I said—one more word—and I'll blow your head off."
女人（惊慌地缩在角落，突然指着机器人）：
"Kick it off! It doesn't eat or drink, but it's still a resource! It can walk!"
K（第一次真正动怒，声音低沉）：
"It consumes nothing. It's just... a companion that doesn't talk back."
老人（突然插嘴，语重心长地）：
"Young man, I understand your attachment to the machine. But a machine is still a machine. We humans—well, as the lord of all creatures—"
K（爆发，猛砸方向盘）：
"Shut up! What do you know?!"
沉默。所有人都被他的爆发震住。
女人（小声地，几乎是在辩解）：
"But... it's a machine... and we're human..."
K（苦笑）：
"Yeah. That's why it'll stay with me to the end. And you—"* 他没说完。
大厅内的沉默开始变异。每个人的呼吸都变得可疑。
K（VO）：
"That was when we started building walls inside our hearts."

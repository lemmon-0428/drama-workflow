──────── 正文 / Script ────────
内景。战车。夜。
INT. THE RIG. NIGHT.
仪表盘的微光是唯一光源，映在每个人明暗不定的脸上。
The dashboard glow is the only light, casting shifting shadows across every face.
K (speaking first, voice utterly flat):
"Whoever drains the most supplies gets off. This is math. Don't dress it up as morality."
K（率先开口，声音毫无波澜）：
“谁最废粮食谁下车。算账的事，别扯什么道义。”
他通过后视镜看向老人。
He looks at the Old Man through the rearview mirror.
OLD MAN (pricked like a needle, instantly hopping mad):
"Me? I'm old bones—how much can I swallow? Besides, the wisdom in this head—"
老人（如被针刺，立刻跳脚）：
“我？我一把老骨头能咽几口唾沫？再说了，我脑子里的活命道理——”
K:
"Wisdom doesn't burn as fuel."
K：
“道理烧不出马力来。”
WOMAN (shrieking):
"We can't throw the old man out! He knows things! He's useful!"
女人（尖叫起来）：
“不能赶大爷走！他懂得多，留着有用！”
K (turning to the Woman, a cold sneer flickering at the corner of his mouth):
"He's useful. So you get off in his place. Fair trade."
K（看向女人，嘴角浮起一丝冷笑）：
“他留着有用，那你滚下去替他？”
女人脸刷地白了，死死咬住嘴唇。
The Woman's face drains white. She bites her lip hard.
MAN (shooting to his feet, head slamming against the roof—THUD—roaring):
"Say that again! One more goddamn word!"
男人（猛地站起身，头“咚”地撞到车顶，怒吼）：
“你他妈再放一句屁试试？！”
K (not turning around, voice like an ice-cold blade):
"Easy there, hero. I'm just running the numbers. Since she insists on staying—maybe she should make herself a little more... useful. For the good of the species."
K（没有回头，声音像淬了冰的刀子）：
“急什么眼啊，护花使者？我只是在算这笔账。既然她非留不可，为了大家能活，她是不是该有点别的‘贡献’……”
话没说完。男人已经把枪死死抵在K的后脑勺上。
He doesn't finish. The Man's gun is already pressed hard against the back of K's skull.
MAN (voice trembling—because K has just stepped on his darkest, most shameful nerve):
"I told you. One more word and I'll splatter your brains all over this dash."
男人（声音在发抖，因为被踩中了最阴暗的尾巴）：
“老子说了，再逼逼一句，脑袋给你轰开花。”
WOMAN (shrinking into the corner in panic, suddenly pointing at the robot like she's grabbed a lifeline):
"Throw the scrap heap out! It's not alive! Dump it to cut weight!"
女人（惊慌地缩在角落，突然指着副驾的机器人，像抓住了救命稻草）：
“让那铁疙瘩滚！它又不是活人！把它丢下去减重！”
K (the first real flare of rage, voice dropping dangerously low):
"It doesn't drink my water. It doesn't burn my fuel. It's just... company that doesn't talk back."
K（第一次真正动怒，声音低沉得可怕）：
“它不喝我的水，不抢我的油，它就是个——不吭声的伙计。”
OLD MAN (cutting in, switching to a patronizing, fatherly tone):
"Young man, I understand—you've got a bond with that scrap heap. But iron can't be worth more than flesh. We're human beings. Living, breathing human beings—"
老人（突然插嘴，换了一副语重心长的口吻）：
“小伙子啊，我知道你跟这铁壳子有感情，可铁的哪有人金贵啊。咱们好歹是人，是活生生的人——”
K (completely erupting, slamming the steering wheel with both fists):
"ALL OF YOU SHUT YOUR MOUTHS! Living, breathing humans?!"
(jabbing a finger at the rearview mirror, eyes savage)
"This pile of scrap has never stabbed me in the back. Not once. You three?! You've been on this rig a few days, and already your eyes are spinning like slot machines—calculating who dies, calculating who's sneaking into whose bed at night!"
K（彻底爆发，猛砸方向盘）：
“都他妈闭嘴！活生生的人？！”
他指着后视镜里的三个人，眼神狠厉：
“这铁壳子没从背后捅过我刀子。你们呢？！上车才几天？眼珠子滴溜溜转，算计着谁该死，算计着晚上谁上谁的床？！”
死寂。所有人都被他撕破脸的爆发震住了。
Dead silence. His outburst has ripped the mask off and stunned them all.
WOMAN (muttering quietly, guiltily):
"But... it's just a thing... we're your own kind..."
女人（小声地，心虚地嘟囔）：
“可……它就是个物件啊……咱们可是同类……”
K (bitter laugh, eyes desolate):
"Yeah. My own kind. That's exactly why it'll outlast me. As for you lot—"
(he doesn't finish the sentence)
K（苦笑，眼神荒凉）：
“是啊，同类。所以它能陪我活到最后。至于你们这帮同类——”
他没往下说。
车内的沉默开始变质。每个人的呼吸声都像是在拉动枪栓。
The silence begins to rot. Every breath sounds like a bolt being racked.
K (V.O.):
"That was when the walls went up. Every word spoken after that was just another brick sealing the tomb."
K（VO）：
“那时候，墙就已经砌起来了。说出口的每一句话，都是堵死坟墓的最后一快砖。”

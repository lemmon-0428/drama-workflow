──────── 正文 / Script ────────
外景。废弃加油站。
EXT. ABANDONED GAS STATION.
锈蚀的油泵像墓碑林立。一个干瘦的老人（70岁，衣袍破烂但洗得干净，眼睛亮得令人不安）站在路中央，双手张开，像在迎接他们。
Rusted fuel pumps stand like tombstones. A gaunt OLD MAN (70s, robes tattered but washed clean, eyes too bright to be sane) stands in the middle of the road, arms spread wide as if welcoming them.
OLD MAN (voice booming, unnaturally loud, tinged with fervor):
"Living souls! Thank the heavens! The sky hasn't gone blind after all!"
老人（声音洪亮得反常，带着某种狂热）：
“活人！感谢老天爷！天上没瞎眼啊！”
WOMAN (leaning forward, excited):
"Another survivor! Stop the car!"
女人（探头，激动）：
“还有活人！快停下！”
K (foot still on the gas):
"We're full. No fuel to spare. No water either."
K（脚下没松油门）：
“满载了。没油也没水。”
WOMAN:
"He's old! Leaving him here is murder!"
女人：
“他一大把年纪了！丢下他就是死！”
MAN (arm tightening around the Woman, grinning at K without warmth):
"Hey, driver. Turning your back on a dying man? That's some bad karma."
男人（搂紧女人，皮笑肉不笑地看向K）：
“我说师傅，见死不救，损阴德啊。”
K (turning, his gaze a blade):
"I told you. This rig can't carry any more dead weight."
K（转头，目光如刀）：
“我说了，这车装不下多余的命。”
MAN (pulling out his gun, not aiming—just weighing it in his palm):
"Then let's vote on it. Three against one. I know how the game works."
男人（掏出枪，没有瞄准，只是在手里掂量）：
“那就举手表决。三比一，规矩我懂。”
沉默。K的视线从枪上移开，重新看向前方。
Silence. K's eyes shift from the gun, back to the road ahead.
K (V.O.):
"They really think this car belongs to them now. Humans... always the same."
K（VO）：
“他们倒真把这车当自己家了。人啊……。”
车门打开。老人敏捷地爬上车，像一条滑溜的鱼。
The door opens. The Old Man scrambles aboard like a slippery fish.
OLD MAN (wedging into the back seat, eyes gleaming, hands clasped):
"Bless you! Bless you both! In a better world, we'd be living in paradise. But no—just like that Tower of Babel business back in the day—"
老人（挤进后排，双眼放光，双手合十）：
“谢天谢地！要不是这事，我们该在天堂一样生活，可惜啊，就跟当年盖那什么巴别塔一样——”
MAN (cutting in, impatient):
"Old bones. Shut your mouth."
男人（不耐烦地打断）：
“老骨头，把嘴闭上。”
OLD MAN (completely unfazed, rambling on with a fervent glow):
"—they were this close to signing a permanent peace treaty. This close! And then—"
老人（毫不在意，继续神叨叨地说着）：
“——眼看就要签下永久和平协议了，就差那么一点啊......——”
K (cutting in, voice cold as steel):
"And ever since then? Nothing but calculation. Human against human."
K（打断，声音冰冷）：
“从此人跟人，只剩下算计。”
车内安静了片刻。机器人发出“嘀”的一声。
A beat of silence. The robot beeps.
OLD MAN (slapping his own thigh, thrilled):
"Exactly! You watching me, me watching you—we're still living in Babel's shadow!"
老人（激动地猛拍大腿）：
“可不就是！你防着我，我盯着你，这不还是活在巴别塔底下吗——”
WOMAN (looking at the Old Man with something like reverence):
"Sir, you really understand things."
女人（崇拜地看着老人）：
“大爷，您活得真明白。”
OLD MAN (eyes burning brighter):
"That's why we survive! To pass on the truth! We live so that—"
老人（越发狂热）：
“所以咱们活着，就是为了传这个理儿——”
K猛踩刹车，车内所有人往前狠狠一倾。
K slams the brakes. Everyone lurches forward violently.
K:
"Pass on what? First, let's see if we even get to keep living."
K：
“传什么？先看看还能不能活吧。”
他盯着仪表盘。上面红色的油量警报灯急促闪烁。
His eyes are fixed on the dash. A red fuel warning light blinks urgently.
K:
"See that? Tank's running dry. Before the next supply point, this rig can only carry three."
K：
“看表。油底壳要干了。到下个废品站之前，这车最多只能拉三口人。”
空气瞬间凝固。
The air turns to glass.

──────── 正文 / Script ────────
内景。战车。行驶中。
INT. THE RIG. STILL MOVING.
灯光忽明忽暗，像这辆车在苟延残喘。
The lights flicker like the rig itself is gasping for life.
画面交叉快速剪辑：
RAPID CROSS-CUTTING:
男人粗暴地摆弄枪械，死死盯着K的后脑。
The Man, roughly fidgeting with his gun, eyes nailed to the back of K's head.
老人神经质地喃喃自语，不停地搓手。
The Old Man, muttering neurotically, hands wringing nonstop.
女人盯着后视镜，神经质地捋着脏兮兮的头发。
The Woman, staring into the rearview mirror, obsessively stroking her filthy hair.
K握方向盘的手骨节泛白，微微发抖。
K's knuckles on the wheel, bone-white, trembling slightly.
机器人发出规律的心跳声，像某种倒计时。
The Robot, emitting a steady heartbeat beep—like a countdown.
OLD MAN (breaking the silence suddenly):
"Such a waste... such a waste... hearts scatter, and the tower crumbles."
老人（突然出声，打破死寂）：
“造孽啊造孽……人心散了，塔就塌了。”
K (cold laugh):
"You seem to have it all figured out. So tell me—what was God really afraid of?"
K（冷笑）：
“你倒是活明白了。那你知道上帝到底怕什么吗？”
OLD MAN (eyes lighting up again, taking the bait):
"Human arrogance! Reaching beyond their station—"
老人（眼睛又亮起来，接茬）：
“怕人太狂妄！不知天高地厚——”
K (enunciating each word like a hammer strike):
"He was afraid we'd stand together."
K（一字一顿）：
“怕我们穿一条裤子。”
老人愣住。
The Old Man freezes.
K:
"Afraid we'd lock arms. Afraid we'd think with one mind. So He tossed a grenade into the crowd—made us watch each other, made us tear each other apart. And then?"
(eyes locked on the rearview mirror)
"He just sat up there, watching us rip into each other like rabid dogs."
K：
“怕咱们抱团。怕大家心往一处想。所以扔个棒槌下来，让人防着人，自己人咬自己人。然后呢？”
他死死盯着后视镜：
“就在天上看着咱们像疯狗一样互咬。”
MAN (snapping, lurching to his feet, gun leveled at K):
"You done?! Who the hell are you really talking about?!"
男人（绷不住了，猛地站起，枪指着K）：
“你没完了是吧？！拐弯抹角骂谁呢？！”
K (spinning around, facing the barrel head-on):
"I'm saying—that gun of yours has been itching to point at the back of my head since day one. Admit it."
K（猛地转过身，直面男人黑洞洞的枪口）：
“我是想说——你那枪，不是早想对着我的后脑勺了吗？”
MAN (finger trembling on the trigger, rage mixed with shame):
"You're the one who was eyeing my woman first—"
男人（手指在扳机上发抖，恼羞成怒）：
“是你他妈先盯上老子女人的——”
K:
"One rough joke and you draw your gun. Well then—"
K：
“一句糙话你就要拔枪。那现在——”
“砰！” / BANG!
枪声在密闭的铁罐头里震耳欲聋。
The gunshot is deafening inside the sealed tin can.
不是男人的枪。
Not the Man's gun.
是K藏在座椅下的枪，不知何时已经攥在手里。
K's gun. Hidden under the seat. Already in his grip.
男人胸口的血窟窿开始冒血。他低头看了看，满脸的难以置信。轰然倒下。
Blood begins to pump from the hole in the Man's chest. He looks down, face frozen in disbelief. He crumples.
WOMAN (piercing scream, throwing herself onto the body):
"NO—!"
女人（惨厉地尖叫，扑向男人）：
“不要——！”
OLD MAN (scrambling into the corner, shaking uncontrollably):
"You... monster... what have you done..."
老人（连滚带爬地缩到角落，抖如筛糠）：
“你……造孽啊……”
K (gun still trailing smoke, voice eerily calm):
"Well. That solves the fuel problem."
K（枪口还在冒着青烟，声音出奇的平静）：
“这下油够烧了。”
突然，女人从男人身下抽出那把没开火的枪，双手发抖，死死对准K。
Suddenly, the Woman pulls the unfired gun from beneath the Man's body, hands shaking, aimed dead at K.
WOMAN (tears streaming, hysterical):
"You animal! You curse God, but you're worse than any beast! It's you—you're the one with poison in your heart!"
女人（泪流满面，歇斯底里）：
“你个畜生！你还骂上帝，你连畜生都不如！是你心里有鬼！”
K (looking at her, not even flinching):
"You're right. I'm rotten too. Not one of us is clean."
K（看着她，连躲都没躲）：
“对。我也烂透了。咱们谁也没比谁干净。”
他缓缓举枪。
He slowly raises his gun.
女人狠狠扣动扳机。（特写：击锤撞空。咔哒。枪里根本没子弹。）
The Woman pulls the trigger hard. (CLOSE-UP: The hammer falls on an empty chamber. CLICK. The gun was never loaded.)
“砰！” / BANG!
第二声实弹枪响。女人倒在血泊中。
Second live shot. The Woman collapses into a pool of blood.
OLD MAN (completely broken, clawing at the window, wailing):
"Dear God! Show yourself! What kind of hell is this—what have we done—!"
老人（崩溃了，疯狂地挠着车窗，哭喊）：
“老天爷啊！显显灵吧！这都造的什么孽啊——”
K (shifting the gun toward the Old Man, the first flicker of struggle crossing his eyes):
"Old man. Every one of your sermons was right. Too bad... no one ever wanted to listen."
K（将枪口移向老人，眼中第一次闪过挣扎）：
“老家伙，你那套大道理都对。可惜啊，没人愿意听。”
“砰！” / BANG!
第三声枪响。
Third shot.

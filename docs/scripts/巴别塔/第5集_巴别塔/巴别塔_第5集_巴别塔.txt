──────── 正文 / Script ────────
车内死一般寂静。浓重的血腥味弥漫。
Deathly silence inside the rig. The thick stench of blood fills the air.
K慢慢转回身，瘫软在驾驶座上。他侧头，看着副驾上的机器人。
K slowly turns back, slumping into the driver's seat. He looks sideways at the Robot beside him.
K (voice hoarse beyond recognition, as if talking to himself):
"Quiet now. Supplies for three, split between one. We might actually make it to the end."
K（声音嘶哑得不成样子，像在自言自语）：
“清净了。三个人的口粮，一个人吃。咱俩能开到终点了。”
机器人注视着他。空洞的金属眼眶里，红光开始闪烁——那是指示灯，此刻却像一颗心脏在搏动。
The Robot watches him. In its hollow metal eye sockets, a red light begins to pulse—an indicator light, but now it beats like a heart.
突然，它第一次主动发出了声音——不是死板的“嘀”，而是一个极不标准的、像是在模仿人类声带摩擦的词汇。它伸出冰冷的机械手，轻轻覆盖在K发抖的手背上。
Then, for the first time, it makes a sound of its own—not a mechanical "beep," but a crude, distorted syllable, as if imitating the friction of human vocal cords. It extends a cold mechanical hand, gently covering K's trembling fingers.
那声音机械、沙沙作响，却透着某种悲悯。
The sound is metallic, crackling with static—yet somehow full of pity.
ROBOT:
"For... give..."
机器人：
“原……谅……”
K彻底僵住了。
K freezes completely.
他缓缓低头，看了一眼自己手里的枪。又看了看机器人。最后抬头看向后视镜里叠在一起的三具尸体。
He slowly looks down at the gun in his hand. Then at the Robot. Then up at the rearview mirror—at the three bodies piled together.
突然，他转头喝了一口汽车汽油，转头看向锈迹斑斑的机器人。
Suddenly, he turns, takes a swig of gasoline from a canister, and looks back at the rust-flecked Robot.
K:
"Tell me... do you think your humans still have a chance? It's been three days since I last drank this lubricant. Any longer and I'm in trouble."
K：
“你说，你们人类还有救吗？三天我没喝这润滑油了，再不喝就麻烦了。”
此时他缓缓打开自己的胸口，那是一个铁灰色的盒子，里面缠绕着各种电线。
He slowly opens his own chest—a gunmetal-gray compartment, tangled with wires.
再检查一番过后，他平静的穿好了衬衣。
After a careful inspection, he calmly buttons his shirt back up.
然后在座位下拿起一个冷冻小盒，里面是各种药剂，随手拉开机器人的胸腔，里面是一个玻璃盒装的跳动心脏。
Then he retrieves a small freezer case from under the seat, filled with various vials. Casually, he opens the Robot's chest cavity—inside is a glass case containing a beating heart.
K:
"If you don't eat something soon, you won't hold up much longer either."
K：
“再不吃点东西，你也挺不住了吧。”
说着把营养液补充进这机器人的躯壳。
He injects nutrient solution into the Robot's body.
K:
"The game continues. Same rules as always. If just one of them—even one—can refuse to abandon this pile of scrap metal that is you... then we let them live."
赌局继续，还是老规矩，但凡有一个人，能不抛弃这机器铁疙瘩的你，我们就让他们活，不然.....
K:
"Otherwise..."
K：
“不然.....”
结尾*
ENDING*
废土荒野的道路上，一辆破旧的小车依旧前行。
On the wasteland road, a battered little rig drives on, still moving forward.
